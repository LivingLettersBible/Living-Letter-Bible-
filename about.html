About Us

Written slowly, read together.

Living Letters started with a simple frustration: reading plans that made the Bible feel like a checklist. We wanted something that felt more like a letter — unhurried, personal, worth returning to.

Our Story

Why "Living Letters"?

The name comes from 2 Corinthians 3, where Paul tells a young, struggling church that they themselves are a letter — not written with ink, but with the Spirit of the living God, not on stone, but on human hearts.

That's the whole idea. Scripture isn't just something we read; it's something that gets written into us, over time, through ordinary days of paying attention. Living Letters is our attempt to build the kind of reading rhythms that let that actually happen — plans short enough to finish, honest enough to trust, and simple enough to return to when life gets loud.

“And we all, with unveiled face, beholding the glory of the Lord, are being transformed into the same image from one degree of glory to another.” 2 Corinthians 3:18
What We Believe

Three things shape everything we make

📖

Rhythm over rules

A plan you can actually keep beats an ambitious one you abandon by day four. We design for return, not guilt.

💬

Honesty over polish

Our devotionals aim to sound like a real conversation about a real text — doubts, tension, and all.

🤝

Together over alone

Every plan and post is written to be shared — with a friend, a small group, or a family at the table.
