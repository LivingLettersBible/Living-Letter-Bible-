What is Living Letters?

Scripture isn't meant to be read alone, or in a hurry.

We build unhurried reading rhythms — short, doable plans paired with reflection — so that Scripture has room to move from the page into daily life. No pressure to catch up, no guilt for missing a day. Just an open invitation back to the text.
