# Vintage line art

These pages come from [WelshPixie/vintageart](https://github.com/WelshPixie/vintageart):
public-domain illustrations from late-1800s and early-1900s publications, traced to SVG and released
under CC0 1.0 (see `LICENSE`). The `school_sewing_*` pieces were traced by Elena "of Valhalla" Grandi.
